Sources, coverage & methodology

2,344 General Fund category records, drawn from FY20–FY27 budget books. Overlapping subtotals are excluded. All 30 available General Fund revenue/expense totals reconcile to the collected source; that is an extraction check, not an audit.

FY20–FY21 budget revenue is missing. FY26 actuals have an unverified cutoff and are excluded from trend defaults. FY27 actuals are unavailable. Published budgets are not independently verified as adopted appropriations. FY22–FY24 PDF covers say “Proposed Version.”

Historical figures use the available source hierarchy. First appearance does not establish a department’s creation date. Revenue and expense differences should not be interpreted as operating surplus or deficit. Department expenses are not a complete account-level general ledger.
